How to play

1. Pick a topic. Like rivers, or history.

2. Type any guess. No question to figure out first - just type any real answer to begin.

3. Check the colour. Green means correct. Redder means further off. Guess again using that clue.

10 guesses and 5 hints per puzzle. New puzzle every day.
