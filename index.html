How to play

Each category has one puzzle a day. Take your first guess blind - that's normal, nobody gets it on guess one. Type a guess and you'll see clues compared against today's answer:

Exact match
Close - same category, not exact
Numbers use a gradient - the closer the shade to green, the closer your guess

You get 10 guesses per puzzle per day, plus 5 optional hints that reveal one letter of the answer at a time. Typos and common alternate names are usually recognised. Come back tomorrow for a new puzzle in each category.
